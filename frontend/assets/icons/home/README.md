# Rental feature illustrations

13 original SVG icons with a shared 96 x 96 canvas, rounded outlines, and warm orange, apricot and cream fills with brown outlines, matching the app's orange accent. Drawn as vectors for sharp rendering on mobile, web and Windows.

- post_listing: listing document, house and add badge
- rent_house: house and rental key
- rent_room: apartment buildings and furnished room
- manage_listings: property clipboard and approved badge
- contracts: contract document and pen
- subscription: membership wallet and coin
- map: folded map and location pin
- assistant: assistant robot and conversation bubble
- introduction: open book and homes
- rules: rules document and balance scales
- guide: handbook and question bubble
- report: safety shield and warning triangle
- contact: headset and conversation bubble

Rendered by HomeFeatureIcon; titles, navigation, and tap actions remain Flutter widgets.
